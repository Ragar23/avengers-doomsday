# Roadmap — Avengers: Doomsday

This game is a reskin. The engine, wave system, ultimates framework and
smoke test all come from `main`; what changed is the cast, the villains, the
palette and the menu.

## What the reskin needed

- **Sprites at the original's fidelity.** The first pass drew everyone on a
  52x48 grid at 2x — 2,496 cells against `scarlet-witch.png`'s 9,408, barely
  a quarter, and every character came out as the same doll in different
  colours. They are now drawn at 104x96 with no upscaling, and the builder
  varies silhouette by `build` (slim, athletic, heavy), hair style, cape and
  helmet, with a `detail` callback for costume work — Cyclops' yellow
  harness and X belt, Thor's armour bands, the witches' robe folds.
  Doctor Doom is drawn front-on rather than through the shared builder: he
  faces the player with both hands charged.
  `tools/pixelfigure.py` holds the shared stride; `tools/make_doomsday.py`
  supplies each character. Regenerate with `python3 tools/make_doomsday.py`.
- **A second tier above that**, for the characters the player actually looks
  at. Hand-drawn art has since replaced Cyclops, Beast, Mystique and Shuri —
  chunky pixel art on a small logical grid, blown up so every pixel is a
  visible block — and at 104x96 the rest read as mush beside it. Thor and the
  Human Torch are now drawn the same way, on a 27x34 grid upscaled x11, by
  `tools/make_hires.py`.
- **A stained-glass backdrop** behind the menu, generated by
  `tools/make_glass.py` in the green of the teaser.
- **Orbitron** for titles, numbers and buttons — the closest free face to the
  squared, cut-cornered DOOMSDAY wordmark. Body copy stays on the Marvel
  face, which is far more readable small. It loads from Google Fonts and
  falls back to the Marvel face if that is unreachable.
- **A darker background.** `images/dd-bg.png` is generated from the original
  `bg.png` — desaturated, dimmed and pushed toward green — so the purple is
  gone without redrawing the scene.
- **A countdown** to 18 December 2026, counting whole calendar months first,
  the way the teaser's clock does.

## Two bugs the reskin exposed in the engine

Both were latent on `main` and would have bitten any future theme:

- `drawMjolnir` and `drawShield` hard-coded `img.mjolnir` and `img.shield`. A
  theme loading neither crashed the render loop on the first frame the weapon
  was in the air. Both now fall back to whatever the hero throws.
- `sess.chosenHero` defaulted to a hero id that no longer existed, so the
  first `resetGame()` read `undefined.sprite`.

A check that every `img.X` reference is actually present in `imageSources`
found both, and is worth running after any asset change.

## Still outstanding

- Boss-wave music, and sound effects tuned to the greener palette.
- The three witches share one hooded silhouette in three colours. Distinct
  silhouettes would read better at speed.
- The Orbitron webfont could not be verified from the build container, whose
  proxy blocks fonts.googleapis.com. It is a plain Google Fonts link and
  degrades to the Marvel face; worth a look in a real browser.
- Touch controls, WASD, and honouring reduced-motion on the canvas — all
  still open on `main` too.
